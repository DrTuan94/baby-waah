Privacy Policy

Baby Waah for iPhone · Last updated: October 25, 2026

In short: Baby Waah does not collect your data. Your photos and your baby's details stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Photos you capture or choose, captions, who is in each photo, milestones, your baby's name and birthday, and your settings are stored only on your device, inside the app. We do not have access to them and they are never uploaded to our servers.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We have no accounts or sign-in.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or third-party tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share your data, because we do not have it.

How the app uses your device

  • Camera: used only when you take a photo inside the app. The photo is saved on your device.
  • Photo library: you choose photos with Apple's photo picker. The app can only see the photos you select.
  • Milestone photos: cutting your baby out of a photo happens on your device using Apple's Vision technology. The photo is not sent anywhere.
  • Face ID or passcode lock: if you turn it on, iOS checks your identity. We never receive or store your biometric data.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, reminders and offers are scheduled on your device by iOS. They are not sent from a server and need no account. You can turn them off in iPhone Settings.
  • Sharing: cards, photos, films and PDFs are created on your device. They leave the app only when you choose to share them, using the iOS share sheet.

Subscriptions

Baby Waah Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and manages the subscription. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. We only learn, on your device, whether your subscription is active.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, the app's data may be included in that backup. Backups are handled by Apple under your own Apple ID settings, not by us.

Children

The app is made for parents and caregivers and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a moment inside the app to remove its photo, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version on this page.
